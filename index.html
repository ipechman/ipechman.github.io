Hey — I'm Ilan, an Aerospace Engineering student at the University of Central Florida. I was born in Rio de Janeiro and have been in the U.S. since I was 13, and somewhere along the way I fell for both rockets and code.

I split my time between engineering — research, CAD, and the machine shop — and software, where I've been going deep on programming and applied AI. I like building things that actually work, learning fast, and problems where hardware meets software.

For my senior design project, I'm working on an electroaerodynamic (EAD) ion-propulsion aircraft. My main focus is aerodynamics, with additional contributions to the team's electronics work.

Off the clock, I'm usually outside with a camera pointed at the night sky. The star trails further down are mine.
